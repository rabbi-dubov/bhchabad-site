About us

Rabbi Levi and Mushky Dubov

We came to Bloomfield Hills in 2017 to build a Jewish home for every Jew here. No membership, no labels, no prior knowledge needed.

Our vision is simple: learning that speaks to your life, holidays you'll remember, and a community that knows your name.

Rabbi Levi Dubov makes deep Jewish ideas clear and practical. He has taught some 40 courses here, his Tanya podcast has passed 40,000 plays in 95 countries, and he is co-author of the Chabad.org Tanya Project with Rabbi Tzvi Freeman.

Mushky Dubov runs the center day to day, leads its women's learning and hosts Shabbat and holiday meals at home. She is a granddaughter of Rabbi Beryl and Batsheva Shemtov, Michigan's first Chabad emissaries.

They live in Bloomfield Hills with their six children.

Our inspiration

The Rebbe

Everything we do grows from the vision of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (1902–1994).

After the Holocaust, the Rebbe sent young couples around the world to care for every Jew they could reach. Thousands answered his call. We are proud to be among them.

“A little light dispels much darkness.”

The Rebbe, teaching from the Tanya

Chabad of Bloomfield Hills is dedicated to the Rebbe, whose love for every Jew inspires all we do.

Our roots

About Chabad-Lubavitch

Chabad-Lubavitch is a worldwide Jewish movement that cares for every Jew, whatever their background.

Chabad is a Hebrew acronym for wisdom, understanding and knowledge: a Judaism of the mind as well as the heart. Lubavitch is the Russian town that was its home for more than a century.

Founded about 250 years ago, it now runs more than 3,500 centers around the world. Ours is one of them.
