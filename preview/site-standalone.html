Welcome

There's a place for you here.

Chabad Jewish Center of Bloomfield Hills is here for every Jew in our community: to learn, to celebrate, and to bring a little more light into the world. Whether you're new to Jewish life or looking to go deeper, you'll find a warm welcome, good company and something meaningful to take home.
